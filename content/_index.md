Hi, I'm **Yanze Wang**, a Ph.D. candidate in Mathematics at the Academy of Mathematics and Systems Science, Chinese Academy of Sciences (AMSS, UCAS), advised by Prof. Yifei Chen. I expect to graduate in June 2027. Here is my [cv](cv.pdf).

My research lies in **birational geometry** — the minimal model program, algebraically integrable foliations, and Moduli spaces. Lately I have also been exploring **AI4Math**, human-verifying AI-generated mathematical arguments and counterexamples.

## Math

I work on the Sarkisov program for algebraically integrable foliations and related questions in the minimal model program. A list of my works is on the [Papers](/papers/) page.

## Code

I also enjoy coding and exploring linux. I've patched my version of dwm, write a hugo theme (with which there are two sites of mine), and vibe-coded small command-line and TUI tools. See the [Projects](/projects/) page (e.g. calman, markerss, cutui, litchung).
