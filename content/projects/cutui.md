# Cutui

A TUI video editor that uses mpv for preview and ffmpeg for export. Inspired by [ninve](https://github.com/...).

It aims to provide a lightweight, keyboard-driven editing experience from the terminal, so you can cut and trim clips without ever leaving your shell.

GitHub: [hiraethecho/cutui](https://github.com/hiraethecho/cutui)
